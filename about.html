Our Mission

Lean Operations.
Competitive Wins.

"We exist to win realistic, profitable federal supply contracts through disciplined execution — not through elaborate marketing or fabricated credentials."

Westforge Supply is purpose-built for the catalog supply segment — the everyday essentials that keep agencies operational: MRO supplies, safety equipment, tools, janitorial products, and warehouse materials. These are repeatable, sourceable, and winnable for a lean small business that executes with precision.

Our approach: identify real opportunities, source from reliable distributors, quote accurately, and deliver on time. No complexity. No overreach. Consistent, professional supply operations.
