Supply Categories

What We Source

⚙️

MRO & Industrial Supplies

Maintenance, repair, and operations materials for industrial and government facilities. Bearings, belts, filters, motors, pumps, and operational consumables sourced through Grainger, MSC Industrial, and Applied Industrial Technologies.

NAICS 423840 · 423850
🛡️

Safety Equipment & PPE

Full-range personal protective equipment: gloves, safety glasses, respirators, hard hats, high-visibility vests, fall protection, and hearing protection. ANSI-rated products sourced through Grainger and Fastenal.

PSC 84 · 85
🔧

Hardware, Fasteners & Tools

Structural hardware, bolts, nuts, screws, anchors, and precision fasteners. Hand tools, power tools, and cutting tools sourced through Fastenal, MSC Industrial, McMaster-Carr, and Zoro.

NAICS 423710 · PSC 51 · 52 · 53
📦

Warehouse & Operational Supplies

Industrial shelving, carts, dollies, material handling equipment, packaging materials, tape, stretch wrap, and operational consumables for warehouse and logistics environments. Sourced through Uline and Global Industrial.

PSC 81
🧹

Janitorial & Facility Supplies

Industrial-grade cleaning chemicals, janitorial equipment, floor care products, restroom supplies, and facility maintenance materials. Sourced through HD Supply and Grainger.

PSC 79 · 72
⚡

Electrical & Plumbing Materials

Wiring, conduit, outlets, switchgear components, pipe fittings, valves, and plumbing hardware for facility maintenance and ongoing operational requirements. Sourced through Grainger and HD Supply.

NAICS 423610 · PSC 47 · 61 · 62
